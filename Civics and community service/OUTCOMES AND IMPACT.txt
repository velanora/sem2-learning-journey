OUTCOMES AND IMPACT:

The Sahulat Card Documentation Aid was carried out in collaboration with Hayatabad Medical Complex (HMC). During the initial execution phase, we organized  120+ physical patient files. Then, we digitalized 250+ patient records using scanning system.This process included careful verification of Documents, removal of duplicate records, and proper arrangement before scanning. By the end, approximately 700 patient files will be digitized, benefiting around 2,750 people including patients, attendants, and staff members like nurses, receptionists, doctors, and IT personnel. This reduced physical handling of files by  staff members such as nurses, doctors, receptionists and IT staff. It also caused faster access to patient information and significantly reduced patient waiting time during record retrieval.This minimized the chances of data loss and ensured more reliable record keeping. This initiative acts as a key step towards the implementation of fully automated hospital management system. Moreover, this improved the workflow efficiency in outpatient and reception areas. This improvement was specifically beneficial in managing high patient flow in busy hospital hours. In the long term, the project saves money previously spent on stationery and files, which can be redirected toward medical equipment.

Apart from the direct immediate outcomes, Our project created several long-term or indirect outcomes. The major impact of this project was that it encouraged the digital transformation in hospital data management system. The project also increased staff awareness about importance of data management. This shift from conventional file system to digitalized files improved the healthcare services and this strengthened the overall trust and satisfaction of patients on the hospital treatment efficiency. Additionally, their staff gained basic exposure to the digital systems, improving their technical confidence.
Students gain practical experience, professional exposure, and teamwork skills through hands-on workflow participation. This experience also enhanced their ability to handle real world challenges in a proper professional manner in a healthcare environment

In terms of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Regarding SDG 3( Good Health and well-being), it reduced risk of infection which contributed in better hygiene and lower risk of infections, delays in treatment of patients due to misplaced files and efficient treatment by faster access to patient records. Under SDG 4 (Quality Education) we created a structured digital dataset useful for medical learning and research. Additionally  it provides hands on experimenceS to students in healthcare documentation system.For SDG 9 ( Industry, Innovation, and infrastructure), our project introduced basic digital transformation in hospital documentation system which improved data organization system of the hospital. It resulted in reduced redundancy in manual filing processes. Our project supports SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and strong Institutions) by enhancing efficiency and transparency within institutional processes. Overall, this project demonstrates how small scale digital initiatives can bring improvements in healthcare department and service delivery.

Recommendations:
We recommend that student volunteers to start early and plan the tasks properly and engage your stakeholders (staff, patients and attendants). Arrange a meeting with the IT department to discuss the stationery and space provided for work. Additionally, ensure the proper data backup and security. Follow our method of dividing work among team members to achieve efficiency. Also, use the advanced tools and software for data management. Students should also learn basic database management systems for better scalability.To gain continuous improvement, regular monitoring and evaluation of the digital system should also be ensured.

The hospital should invest in digital infrastructure and for sustainability. In order to achieve innovative solutions, HMC should collaborate with different institutions to get benefit from the creativity of students. Moreover, they should maintain  strict data privacy and confidentiality policies. In the future, this digital records can help transition from basic digitization to a fully integrated electronic health record (EHR) system and such systems can be integrated with national health databases to improve coordination across hospitals.
